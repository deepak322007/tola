How to read this

  1. Prices are adjusted for contract size and gold purity.
  2. Only contracts with nearby expiry dates are compared.
  3. Signals use earlier sessions; simulated trades begin at the next close.
  4. Estimated costs are included for both sides of a trade.

A quiet monitor is a valid result. Activity is not market depth.
