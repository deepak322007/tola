Is the small gold contract priced like the big one?

GOLDM, GOLDTEN, GOLDGUINEA and GOLDPETAL are the same metal in different sizes. TOLA puts them on one footing (₹ per 10 g of pure gold) and asks whether the gaps between them survive trading costs. Often they don't, and the page will say so.

Ground rules

  1. Same units first. Quote base and purity are removed before anything is compared.
  2. Expiry stays in view. Contracts are paired by nearest expiry; there is no stitched front-month series.
  3. Only yesterday's evidence. A signal today uses earlier days only, and is traded at the following close.
  4. Both legs pay. Costs are charged on each side of every position change.

A quiet monitor is a valid result. Volume is not depth.
